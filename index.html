02

Customer Support AI Assistant

Client project — live fashion e-commerce site (confidential)
n8n · Gemini

The challenge

Visitors had product questions with no one available to answer instantly, and interested buyers had no structured way to start an order — every missed question was a missed sale.

The solution

A Gemini-powered assistant that understands product questions, answers them directly, and — when a visitor wants to order — collects name, phone, and email, logs the lead to Google Sheets, and emails the team instantly so follow-up can start.

n8nGoogle GeminiGoogle SheetsEmail Automation
Business value: reduces repetitive support workload while capturing every serious buyer instead of losing them to an unanswered question.

03

AI-Powered Lead Management & Qualification System

Personal automation project
New · n8n

The challenge

Businesses receive leads from different sources, but managing them manually causes delays, incomplete information, inconsistent follow-ups, and high-priority leads slipping through the cracks.

The solution

An automated workflow: a lead comes in through a webhook, the data is validated, an AI agent analyzes needs, interest level, and intent, then classifies the lead as High, Medium, or Low priority — storing it in Google Sheets and triggering an immediate notification.

n8nWebhookGoogle GeminiGoogle SheetsAPIs
Business value: helps businesses respond faster to high-value leads and keep lead data organized without manual sorting.
